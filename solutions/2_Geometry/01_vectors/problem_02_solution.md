# Exercise 2. Distance as the Length of a Vector

## Given

$$
A=(1,2,3)
$$

$$
B=(4,-2,6)
$$

## 1. Construct the Vector \(\overrightarrow{AB}\)

The vector from point \(A\) to point \(B\) is found by subtracting the coordinates of \(A\) from the coordinates of \(B\).

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=B-A
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=(4,-2,6)-(1,2,3)
$$

Subtract the corresponding coordinates:

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=(4-1,-2-2,6-3)
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{\overrightarrow{AB}=(3,-4,3)}
$$

## 2. Compute the Length of \(\overrightarrow{AB}\)

The length of a vector \(\mathbf{v}=(v_1,v_2,v_3)\) is

$$
\|\mathbf{v}\|=\sqrt{v_1^2+v_2^2+v_3^2}
$$

Substitute \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(3,-4,3)\):

$$
\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|=\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2+3^2}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{9+16+9}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{34}
$$

Therefore, the length of the vector is

$$
\boxed{\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|=\sqrt{34}}
$$

## 3. Compute the Distance Using the Coordinate Formula

The distance between two points in three-dimensional space is

$$
d(A,B)=\sqrt{(x_B-x_A)^2+(y_B-y_A)^2+(z_B-z_A)^2}
$$

Substitute \(A=(1,2,3)\) and \(B=(4,-2,6)\):

$$
d(A,B)=\sqrt{(4-1)^2+(-2-2)^2+(6-3)^2}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2+3^2}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{9+16+9}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{34}
$$

Therefore, the distance between the points is

$$
\boxed{d(A,B)=\sqrt{34}}
$$

Approximately,

$$
d(A,B)\approx5.83
$$

## 4. Why Do Both Calculations Give the Same Result?

The vector \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) describes the displacement from point \(A\) to point \(B\).

Its components are the coordinate differences:

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=(x_B-x_A,y_B-y_A,z_B-z_A)
$$

The length of this vector is

$$
\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|
=\sqrt{(x_B-x_A)^2+(y_B-y_A)^2+(z_B-z_A)^2}
$$

This is exactly the coordinate formula for the distance between \(A\) and \(B\).

Therefore, the distance between two points equals the length of the vector connecting them.

$$
\boxed{d(A,B)=\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|=\sqrt{34}}
$$

## Final Answers

- **Vector:** \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(3,-4,3)\)
- **Vector length:** \(\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|=\sqrt{34}\)
- **Distance between the points:** \(d(A,B)=\sqrt{34}\approx5.83\)
- **Conclusion:** The distance between two points is the length of their difference vector.
