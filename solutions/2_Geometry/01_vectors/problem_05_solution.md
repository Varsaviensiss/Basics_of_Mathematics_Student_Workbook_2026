# Exercise 5. Angle Between Vectors

## Given

$$
\mathbf{u}=(1,1,0),\qquad \mathbf{v}=(1,0,1)
$$

## 1. Predict the Type of Angle Using the Dot Product

Calculate the dot product:

$$
\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=1(1)+1(0)+0(1)
$$

$$
=1+0+0=1
$$

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=1>0}
$$

Since the dot product is positive, the angle between the vectors is acute.

## 2. Compute the Angle

The angle formula is

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}||\mathbf{v}|}
$$

Find the lengths:

$$
|\mathbf{u}|=\sqrt{1^2+1^2+0^2}=\sqrt2
$$

$$
|\mathbf{v}|=\sqrt{1^2+0^2+1^2}=\sqrt2
$$

Substitute:

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\sqrt2}
$$

$$
\cos\theta=\frac12
$$

Therefore,

$$
\theta=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac12\right)
$$

$$
\boxed{\theta=60^\circ=\frac{\pi}{3}}
$$

## 3. Compare the Result with the Prediction

The calculated angle is \(60^\circ\), which is less than \(90^\circ\).

Therefore, the angle is acute, as predicted by the positive dot product.

$$
\boxed{\text{Prediction confirmed: the angle is acute.}}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=1,\qquad \theta=60^\circ}
$$
