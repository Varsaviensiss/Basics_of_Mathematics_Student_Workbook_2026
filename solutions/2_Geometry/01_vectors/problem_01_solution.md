# Exercise 1. Vector Operations and Their Meaning

## Given

$$
\mathbf{u}=(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
\mathbf{v}=(-1,4,2)
$$

## 1. Compute \(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}\)

Add the corresponding components:

$$
\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}=(2,-1,3)+(-1,4,2)
$$

$$
=(2+(-1),-1+4,3+2)
$$

$$
=(1,3,5)
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}=(1,3,5)}
$$

## 2. Compute \(\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}\)

Subtract the corresponding components:

$$
\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}=(2,-1,3)-(-1,4,2)
$$

$$
=(2-(-1),-1-4,3-2)
$$

$$
=(3,-5,1)
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}=(3,-5,1)}
$$

## 3. Compute \(3\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v}\)

First, multiply each vector by its scalar:

$$
3\mathbf{u}=3(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
=(6,-3,9)
$$

$$
2\mathbf{v}=2(-1,4,2)
$$

$$
=(-2,8,4)
$$

Now subtract the vectors:

$$
3\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v}=(6,-3,9)-(-2,8,4)
$$

$$
=(6-(-2),-3-8,9-4)
$$

$$
=(8,-11,5)
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{3\mathbf{u}-2\mathbf{v}=(8,-11,5)}
$$

## 4. Verify That \((\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})-\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{u}\)

From Part 1:

$$
\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}=(1,3,5)
$$

Subtract \(\mathbf{v}\):

$$
(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})-\mathbf{v}=(1,3,5)-(-1,4,2)
$$

$$
=(1-(-1),3-4,5-2)
$$

$$
=(2,-1,3)
$$

Since

$$
\mathbf{u}=(2,-1,3)
$$

we obtain

$$
\boxed{(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})-\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{u}}
$$

The identity is verified.

## 5. Geometric Meaning of Vector Addition

Adding two vectors means combining their displacements.

**Head-to-tail method:**

1. Draw \(\mathbf{u}\) from an initial point.
2. Place the tail of \(\mathbf{v}\) at the head of \(\mathbf{u}\), without changing its length or direction.
3. Draw the resultant vector \(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}\) from the initial point of \(\mathbf{u}\) to the final point of \(\mathbf{v}\).

For the given vectors,

$$
\mathbf{u}=(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
\mathbf{v}=(-1,4,2)
$$

the resultant is

$$
\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}=(1,3,5)
$$

Geometrically, the combined displacement is \(1\) unit in the positive \(x\)-direction, \(3\) units in the positive \(y\)-direction, and \(5\) units in the positive \(z\)-direction.

Vector addition can also be represented by the **parallelogram rule**: when both vectors start at the same point, their sum is the diagonal of the parallelogram formed by the two vectors.
