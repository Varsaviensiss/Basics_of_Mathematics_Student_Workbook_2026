# Exercise 2. A Line in Space

## Given

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2)
$$

## 1. Identify a Point and a Direction Vector

The point in the vector equation is

$$
\boxed{P_0=(2,-1,3)}
$$

The direction vector is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{v}=(1,2,-2)}
$$

The parametric equations are

$$
x=2+t
$$

$$
y=-1+2t
$$

$$
z=3-2t
$$

## 2. Check Whether \(P=(4,3,-1)\) Lies on the Line

From the \(x\)-coordinate:

$$
4=2+t
$$

$$
t=2
$$

Check the \(y\)-coordinate:

$$
y=-1+2(2)=3
$$

This matches the given \(y\)-coordinate.

Check the \(z\)-coordinate:

$$
z=3-2(2)=-1
$$

This also matches the given \(z\)-coordinate.

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{P=(4,3,-1)\text{ lies on }L}
$$

## 3. Find the Point Corresponding to \(t=-1\)

Substitute \(t=-1\):

$$
x=2+(-1)=1
$$

$$
y=-1+2(-1)=-3
$$

$$
z=3-2(-1)=5
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{(1,-3,5)}
$$

## Final Answer

- **Point on the line:** \((2,-1,3)\)
- **Direction vector:** \((1,2,-2)\)
- **Membership check:** \(P=(4,3,-1)\) lies on the line at \(t=2\).
- **Point at \(t=-1\):** \((1,-3,5)\)
