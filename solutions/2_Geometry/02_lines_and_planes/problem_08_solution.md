# Exercise 8. Intersection Point of a Line and a Plane

## Given

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(1,0,2)+t(1,2,-1)
$$

$$
\Pi:x+y+z=4
$$

## 1. Write the Parametric Equations

$$
x=1+t
$$

$$
y=2t
$$

$$
z=2-t
$$

## 2. Substitute into the Plane Equation

$$
x+y+z=4
$$

$$
(1+t)+2t+(2-t)=4
$$

Combine like terms:

$$
3+2t=4
$$

$$
2t=1
$$

$$
\boxed{t=\frac12}
$$

## 3. Find the Intersection Point

Substitute \(t=\frac12\) into each coordinate:

$$
x=1+\frac12=\frac32
$$

$$
y=2\left(\frac12\right)=1
$$

$$
z=2-\frac12=\frac32
$$

Therefore, the intersection point is

$$
\boxed{Q=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right)}
$$

## 4. Check the Point on the Line

Using \(t=\frac12\):

$$
(1,0,2)+\frac12(1,2,-1)
$$

$$
=\left(1+\frac12,0+1,2-\frac12\right)
$$

$$
=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right)
$$

The point satisfies the line equation.

## 5. Check the Point on the Plane

$$
\frac32+1+\frac32=4
$$

$$
3+1=4
$$

$$
4=4
$$

The point also satisfies the plane equation.

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{Q=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right)}
$$
