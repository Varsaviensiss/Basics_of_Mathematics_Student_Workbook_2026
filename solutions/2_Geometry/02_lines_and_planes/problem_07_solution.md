# Exercise 7. A Line Relative to a Plane

## Given

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,-1,0)
$$

$$
\Pi:x+y+z=2
$$

## 1. Identify the Direction and Normal Vectors

The line's direction vector is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{v}=(1,-1,0)}
$$

The plane's normal vector is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{n}=(1,1,1)}
$$

## 2. Compute the Dot Product

$$
\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{n}
=(1,-1,0)\cdot(1,1,1)
$$

$$
=1-1+0
$$

$$
=0
$$

Thus, the line's direction vector is perpendicular to the plane's normal vector.

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{n}=0}
$$

This means the line is parallel to the plane or lies entirely within it.

## 3. Check Whether a Point on the Line Lies in the Plane

Use the point at \(t=0\):

$$
P_0=(1,1,0)
$$

Substitute it into the plane equation:

$$
1+1+0=2
$$

The point lies in the plane.

Since the line's direction is parallel to the plane and one point on the line lies in the plane, the entire line lies in the plane.

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{L\subset\Pi}
$$

The line lies entirely in the plane.
