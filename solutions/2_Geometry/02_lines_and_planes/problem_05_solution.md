# Exercise 5. Relative Position of Two Lines

## Given

$$
L_1:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,2,1)
$$

$$
L_2:(x,y,z)=(2,0,1)+s(0,1,-1)
$$

## 1. Write the Parametric Equations

For \(L_1\):

$$
x=1+t,\quad y=1+2t,\quad z=t
$$

For \(L_2\):

$$
x=2,\quad y=s,\quad z=1-s
$$

## 2. Check Whether the Lines Intersect

If the lines intersect, their coordinates must be equal.

Equating the \(x\)-coordinates:

$$
1+t=2
$$

$$
t=1
$$

Equating the \(y\)-coordinates:

$$
1+2t=s
$$

Substitute \(t=1\):

$$
s=1+2(1)=3
$$

Now check the \(z\)-coordinates.

For \(L_1\):

$$
z=t=1
$$

For \(L_2\):

$$
z=1-s=1-3=-2
$$

Since

$$
1\neq-2
$$

the lines do not intersect.

## 3. Determine Whether the Lines Are Parallel

Their direction vectors are

$$
\mathbf{v}_1=(1,2,1)
$$

$$
\mathbf{v}_2=(0,1,-1)
$$

These vectors are not scalar multiples because the first component of \(\mathbf{v}_1\) is \(1\), while the first component of \(\mathbf{v}_2\) is \(0\).

Therefore, the lines are not parallel.

## Final Classification

The lines neither intersect nor are parallel. Hence, they are skew lines.

$$
\boxed{L_1\text{ and }L_2\text{ are skew}}
$$
