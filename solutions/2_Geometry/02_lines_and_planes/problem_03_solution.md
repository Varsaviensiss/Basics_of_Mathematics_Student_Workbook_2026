# Exercise 3. Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

## Given

$$
P=(1,-2,3),\qquad \mathbf{n}=(2,1,-1)
$$

## 1. Find the Plane Equation

The general equation of a plane is

$$
ax+by+cz=d
$$

Its normal vector is \((a,b,c)\). Therefore,

$$
2x+y-z=d
$$

Substitute the point \(P=(1,-2,3)\):

$$
2(1)+(-2)-3=d
$$

$$
2-2-3=d
$$

$$
d=-3
$$

Therefore, the plane equation is

$$
\boxed{2x+y-z=-3}
$$

or, in general form,

$$
\boxed{2x+y-z+3=0}
$$

## 2. Check the Given Point

Substitute \(P=(1,-2,3)\):

$$
2(1)+(-2)-3+3=0
$$

$$
2-2-3+3=0
$$

$$
0=0
$$

The point satisfies the plane equation.

## 3. Explain the Role of the Normal Vector

The normal vector is perpendicular to every direction lying in the plane. It determines the plane's orientation.

The point \(P\) determines the plane's position, while the normal vector determines its orientation.

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{2x+y-z+3=0}
$$
