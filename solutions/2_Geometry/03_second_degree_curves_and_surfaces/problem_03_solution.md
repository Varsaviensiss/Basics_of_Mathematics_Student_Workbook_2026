# Exercise 3. Ellipse

## Given

$$
\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1
$$

## 1. Find the Semiaxis Lengths

The standard form of an ellipse centered at the origin is

$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1
$$

Comparing the given equation with the standard form:

$$
a^2=25
$$

$$
a=\sqrt{25}=5
$$

$$
b^2=9
$$

$$
b=\sqrt{9}=3
$$

Therefore, the semiaxis lengths are

$$
\boxed{a=5,\qquad b=3}
$$

The major semiaxis is horizontal because \(25>9\).

## 2. Find the Intercepts with the Coordinate Axes

### x-intercepts

Set \(y=0\):

$$
\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{0^2}{9}=1
$$

$$
\frac{x^2}{25}=1
$$

$$
x^2=25
$$

$$
x=\pm5
$$

Therefore, the x-intercepts are

$$
\boxed{(-5,0)\text{ and }(5,0)}
$$

### y-intercepts

Set \(x=0\):

$$
\frac{0^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1
$$

$$
\frac{y^2}{9}=1
$$

$$
y^2=9
$$

$$
y=\pm3
$$

Therefore, the y-intercepts are

$$
\boxed{(0,-3)\text{ and }(0,3)}
$$

## 3. Sketch the Ellipse

The ellipse is centered at the origin:

$$
\boxed{C=(0,0)}
$$

Its vertices and co-vertices are

$$
(-5,0),\quad (5,0),\quad (0,-3),\quad (0,3)
$$

Plot these four points and connect them with a smooth, closed oval.

```text
                    y
                    ^
                    |
                 (0,3)
                    *
               .         .
            .               .
(-5,0) *---------+-----------* (5,0)  ---> x
            .    (0,0)       .
               .         .
                    *
                 (0,-3)
                    |
```

The sketch is wider horizontally than it is vertically.

## 4. Along Which Axis Is the Ellipse More Stretched?

The ellipse is more stretched along the **x-axis**.

This is visible directly from the equation:

$$
\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1
$$

The denominator under \(x^2\) is \(25\), while the denominator under \(y^2\) is \(9\).

$$
25>9
$$

Therefore, the ellipse extends \(5\) units to the left and right, but only \(3\) units upward and downward.

$$
\boxed{\text{Horizontal semiaxis}=5}
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Vertical semiaxis}=3}
$$

## Final Answer

- **Center:** \((0,0)\)
- **Horizontal semiaxis length:** \(5\)
- **Vertical semiaxis length:** \(3\)
- **x-intercepts:** \((-5,0)\) and \((5,0)\)
- **y-intercepts:** \((0,-3)\) and \((0,3)\)
- **Major axis:** x-axis
- **Shape:** A horizontally stretched ellipse
