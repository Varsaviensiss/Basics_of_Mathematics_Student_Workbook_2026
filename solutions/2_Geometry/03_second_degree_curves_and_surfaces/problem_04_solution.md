# Exercise 4. Parabola

## Given

$$
y^2=12x
$$

## 1. Identify the Vertex

The standard form of a horizontal parabola is

$$
(y-k)^2=4p(x-h)
$$

The vertex is \((h,k)\).

Comparing with the given equation:

$$
(y-0)^2=12(x-0)
$$

$$
h=0,\qquad k=0
$$

Therefore, the vertex is

$$
\boxed{V=(0,0)}
$$

## 2. Find the Axis of Symmetry

The equation contains \(y^2\), so the parabola is symmetric about the x-axis.

Therefore, its axis of symmetry is

$$
\boxed{y=0}
$$

## 3. Determine the Opening Direction

Rewrite the equation:

$$
y^2=12x
$$

$$
x=\frac{y^2}{12}
$$

Since \(y^2\geq 0\), we have

$$
x\geq 0
$$

Therefore, the parabola opens to the right.

$$
\boxed{\text{Opening direction: right}}
$$

## 4. Find Points When \(x=3\)

Substitute \(x=3\) into the equation:

$$
y^2=12(3)
$$

$$
y^2=36
$$

Taking the square root of both sides:

$$
y=\pm\sqrt{36}
$$

$$
y=\pm6
$$

Therefore, the two points are

$$
\boxed{(3,6)\text{ and }(3,-6)}
$$

## 5. Sketch the Parabola

Plot the vertex and the two points:

$$
V=(0,0)
$$

$$
P_1=(3,6),\qquad P_2=(3,-6)
$$

Draw a smooth curve opening to the right and symmetric about the x-axis.

```text
                 y
                 ^
                 |
                 |                 *
                 |              (3,6)
                 |           .'
                 |        .'
-----------------V------------------------> x
              (0,0)     .'
                 |        `.
                 |           `.
                 |              *
                 |             (3,-6)
```

The sketch is symmetric across the x-axis, and both points lie on the vertical line \(x=3\).

## Final Answer

- **Vertex:** \((0,0)\)
- **Axis of symmetry:** \(y=0\) (the x-axis)
- **Opening direction:** Right
- **Points when \(x=3\):** \((3,6)\) and \((3,-6)\)
