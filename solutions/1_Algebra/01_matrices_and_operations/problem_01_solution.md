# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

Given:

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3\\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0\\
-2 & 3\\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## 1. Sizes of Matrices \(A\) and \(B\)

The size of a matrix is given by:

$$
\text{Number of rows}\times\text{Number of columns}
$$

Matrix \(A\) has 2 rows and 3 columns:

$$
\boxed{A\text{ is a }2\times3\text{ matrix}}
$$

Matrix \(B\) has 3 rows and 2 columns:

$$
\boxed{B\text{ is a }3\times2\text{ matrix}}
$$

## 2. Find the Entries

In matrix notation, \(a_{ij}\) denotes the entry in row \(i\), column \(j\).

For matrix \(A\):

$$
a_{12}=-1
$$

$$
a_{23}=5
$$

For matrix \(B\):

$$
b_{21}=-2
$$

$$
b_{32}=1
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{a_{12}=-1,\quad a_{23}=5}
$$

$$
\boxed{b_{21}=-2,\quad b_{32}=1}
$$

## 3. Write the Row and Column as Vectors

The second row of matrix \(A\) is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

As a row vector:

$$
\boxed{(0,\ 4,\ 5)}
$$

The first column of matrix \(B\) is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
-2\\
4
\end{pmatrix}
$$

As a column vector:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
-2\\
4
\end{pmatrix}}
$$

## Final Answers

1. \(A\) is \(2\times3\), and \(B\) is \(3\times2\).
2. \(a_{12}=-1,\ a_{23}=5,\ b_{21}=-2,\ b_{32}=1\).
3. The second row of \(A\) is \((0,4,5)\), and the first column of \(B\) is \(\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\4\end{pmatrix}\).
