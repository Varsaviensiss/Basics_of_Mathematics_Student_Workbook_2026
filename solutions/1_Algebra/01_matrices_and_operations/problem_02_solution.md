# Exercise 2: Addition and Scalar Multiplication

Given

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2\\
-1&3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}
4&-2\\
0&5
\end{pmatrix}
\]

## 1. Calculate \(A+B\)

\[
A+B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1+4&2+(-2)\\
-1+0&3+5
\end{pmatrix}
\]

\[
\boxed{A+B=\begin{pmatrix}
5&0\\
-1&8
\end{pmatrix}}
\]

## 2. Calculate \(A-B\)

\[
A-B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1-4&2-(-2)\\
-1-0&3-5
\end{pmatrix}
\]

\[
\boxed{A-B=\begin{pmatrix}
-3&4\\
-1&-2
\end{pmatrix}}
\]
